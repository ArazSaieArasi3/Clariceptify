# R1 scope and claim boundary

Decision date: 2026-10-24. Status: adopted for the R1 preparation scope. Evidence: issue [#2](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/Clariceptify/issues/2).

Clariceptify represents knowledge about purposeful conceptualization: agents, mental states, conceptual activity, explicit conceptual content, representations, referents, organization, constraints, evaluation and the provenance of conceptual decisions. It does not attempt a universal theory of cognition.

## Selected input

The selected specification, revision 1 dated 24 October 2026, contains **137 concepts**, **20 subject domains plus a Foundations support layer**, **174 associations**, **122 generalizations**, and **24 textual interpretation constraints**. Foundations contains six concepts. Each concept has one primary catalogue domain; cross-domain relations are allowed. A subject domain is not automatically an independently adequate ontology module.

The ORSD contains 30 functional requirements and 30 associated CQs, ten quality/delivery requirements, and eight separately deferred requirements with eight exploratory CQs. It was reconstructed after the model. These are not evidence of independent prospective stakeholder elicitation or requirement satisfaction.

Names of the twenty domains remain unchanged for R1. Uneven domain sizes, including single-concept Ontology and Knowledge domains, are accepted scope debt. Counts describe the input, not a permanent model invariant: any correction requires an explicit delta and impact assessment.

## Included release families

The eventual v0.1.0 package comprises the selected conceptual registries, requirements/CQs, interpretation constraints, source pointers and qualifications, synchronized diagrams and readable specifications, typed traceability, declared checks, a minimal example/counterexample, release metadata and known limitations. Their presence and checks are established by their individual work items, not by adopting this scope decision.

## Required distinctions

Process/product; mental state/content/expression; description/referent; content/representation; type/instance; criterion/evaluation/result. Conceptualization is treated as purposeful activity with explicit conceptual outputs; statements about all human cognition are outside the claim.

## Exclusions and deferred work

The complete 387-record inventory, 419-entry working catalogue, 37-domain formal development, Workbench, private application material and whole thesis/manuscript are not migrated wholesale. Engineering, research-method and application ontologies are not added as subject domains. Full OWL/SHACL/FOL implementation, a relational projection, independent expert validation, public documentation sites, a final paper and DOI issuance are not R1 delivery prerequisites.

## Release claim

After the relevant gates actually pass, R1 may claim a versioned, reconstructable conceptual-model and requirements baseline with the declared traceability and checks. It may not claim universal completeness, first-ever novelty, all-CQ satisfaction, full-text source verification, joint satisfiability of all constraints, native-tool OntoUML conformance or independent expert acceptance without the corresponding evidence. Connectivity and syntax checks alone cannot establish these claims.

The alpha.1 station records contracts, schemas and input/migration preparation. It is not the full conceptual-model release. Source qualifications and missing human review remain visible. Later R2 assesses scoped representational adequacy using questions, examples and counterexamples without a class-count quota; R3 formalizes a declared subset.

This decision adopts the scope authorized in the project discussion; it records no independent semantic approval.
