# Purpose and scope

The ontology describes knowledge about explicit, purposeful conceptualization.
It connects agents and mental orientation to activities, explicit conceptual
content, representations, interpretation, evaluation and the provenance of choices.
It does not encompass every form of cognition or establish universal philosophical
agreement about concepts.

R1 includes 137 concepts, twenty subject domains plus Foundations, 174 associations,
122 generalizations and 24 textual constraints. Counts describe the selected input.
Each concept has one primary catalogue domain; cross-domain relations remain allowed.

The owner accepted the exact documented R1 package and its limitations. That scope
acceptance is distinct from independent expert validation or all-CQ satisfaction.
Definitions, typing and imported support remain candidate scientific commitments.
