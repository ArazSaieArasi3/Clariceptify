# Requirements and competency questions

The ORSD was reconstructed after the model. It includes 30 functional requirements
and 30 principal CQ contracts, ten quality/delivery requirements and eight deferred
needs with eight exploratory CQs. These are not prospective stakeholder agreement.

Each principal CQ links to a functional requirement, required answer fields,
acceptance and adverse cases. [Candidate witnesses](../../traceability/requirements-model.yaml)
connect the contracts to concept/domain IDs while retaining satisfaction
NOT_ASSESSED, execution NOT_EXECUTED and answers NOT_COMPUTED.

The complete [requirement sources](../../requirements/README.md) and
[CQ contracts](../../requirements/competency-questions.yaml) are available.
The portal presents the full thirty-pair table without promoting the states.

The [positive](../examples/conceptualization-positive.md) and
[negative](../examples/conceptualization-negative.md) fixtures exercise one selected
subject-union clause. Their actual accept/reject results do not execute all CQs
or all 24 constraints. Later R2 reviews the thirty principal CQs in three bounded
batches; executable formal queries require the declared R3 slice.
