# Sources and support qualifications

The [source register](../reference/sources.md) contains 53 imported references.
The [support registry](../../evidence/concept-support.yaml) binds 137 concepts to
inherited screening qualifications: 44 D, 83 B and 10 P. These labels distinguish
direct discussion, theoretical basis and pending support within the recorded import.
They do not establish freshly verified passages or endorsement of project typing.

Each concept reference preserves source numbers and qualifications. Use the
[full bibliography](../../evidence/bibliography.md) and exact source locators before
interpreting a citation as support for a specific modeling choice.

The [related-work register](../../evidence/related-work/candidate-register.yaml)
contains 19 recovered work labels, not 19 verified distinct ontologies.
The [critical-review protocol](../../evidence/related-work/review-protocol.yaml)
fixes subsequent retrieval, full-text and count definitions. Its existence is not
completed review. Third-party full texts and private raw inputs are excluded from
publication; attribution and uncertainty remain visible.
