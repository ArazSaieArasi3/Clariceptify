The central bounded finding is that inspected prior work already covers many parts of agency, intentionality, context, representation, decisions, time and evidence. A **single native, tested integration** of a dated revisable conceptual choice among alternatives, responsible actors, intended/successful reference, cited evidence, rationale and viewpoint was not established across those selected versions. This is a research question about integration and evaluation. It is not evidence that no ontology elsewhere does this or that the present model already does it well.
