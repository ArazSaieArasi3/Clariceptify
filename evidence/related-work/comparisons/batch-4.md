## Shared case and adverse case

Ada observes a patient's pulse E, records document M, uses construct K and makes conceptual decision D at T with evidence V and justification J. An IAO information content entity may document part of this, a Cognitive Atlas construct supplies a cognitive term, and EFO's subject-relative concept/conception distinguishes Ada's thought from the possible target. The model identifiers in the matrix are **questions for mapping**, not equivalences.

At T2, Ada learns that her original target identification was mistaken. A mixed Cognitive Atlas construct/contrast identifier and a contested measurement edge do not settle the reference. An EFO concept representing target concepts of opposing reference status would inherit both under A14/A15 and normalized D1/D2; this is a conditional source-level counterexample, not a proof that the whole theory is inconsistent. The selected IAO aboutness axiom does not itself identify the successful observed target or Ada's rationale. This case is a reviewer construction, not an executed cross-ontology test.
