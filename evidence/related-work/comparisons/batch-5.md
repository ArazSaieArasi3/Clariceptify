## One common case and one adverse case

Ada at T observes a patient pulse E, forms concept K, writes expression X with form F and content C using method M, and records decision D citing evidence V and rationale J. A later physical copy P realizes the form without becoming K, C or E. This reviewer case lets the three sources contribute different parts; it is not a combined executable theory.

At T2 Ada learns that E was misidentified and revises D. AdultPerson and MinorPerson may share CMO's Person primary anchor while an adult instance remains outside MinorPerson; conceptual broader does not imply subclass. Two different encoding methods on the same F/C pair conflict with selected Representation Ax9. Literal COM D1/D4 creates a percept-classification concern. These are separate conceptual and source-level adverse cases, not cross-ontology reasoner results.
