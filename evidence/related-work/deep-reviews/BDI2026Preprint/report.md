### D2_problem_contribution

The work proposes a reusable BDI ODP for semantically interoperable agency, process-oriented state dynamics, temporal validity and a declarative/procedural bridge. It extends a preliminary T2B2T version rather than originating all BDI concepts. The overlap with mental-state processes, goals, plans and explanatory traces is substantial; reported LAG/SEMAS use does not prove general rationality, conceptualization completeness or corpus-wide novelty. [REVIEWER_INTERPRETATION; E02, E05, E06, E13, E14, E15]
