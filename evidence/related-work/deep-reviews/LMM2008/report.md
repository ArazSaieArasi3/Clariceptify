### D2_problem_contribution

The gap is interoperability between heterogeneous lexical resources and ontology-based interpretation. The proposed mechanism separates expression, meaning and reference while reusing contextual descriptions, situations and lexical alignments. This directly overlaps conceptualization concerns; novelty cannot be claimed merely from those three distinctions. [REVIEWER_INTERPRETATION; E01, E03, E04, E08, E11, E14]
