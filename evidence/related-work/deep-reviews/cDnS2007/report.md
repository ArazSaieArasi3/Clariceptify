## Common review dimensions

### D1_identity_coverage

The selected primary text is the three-author2007 Dagstuhl work,40 pages. Reading covers all sections,141 numbered displays, Table 1, both figures and notes/references. The one-author2008 journal text remains unavailable in #263; current OWL 1.1 is not silently equated with this text. [AUTHOR_REPORTED; E01, E23, A01]

### D2_problem_contribution

The problem is explicit representation of social agents and collectives together with the descriptions, plans and norms that confer their unity. The constructive contribution treats contextual conceptualization as a first-class social/informational/circumstantial/epistemic structure and develops increasingly rich collective types. This is strong prior conceptualization coverage, not evidence of universal adequacy. [AUTHOR_REPORTED; E01, E03, E19, E21, E22]

### D3_method

Constructive formal-ontology development extends previous DnS/collective work. The authors give informal principles, signatures, composition definitions, worked legal/scientific examples and a grounded extension. Most computational details and complete axiom reports are referred elsewhere; richer plans and norm theories are deliberately out of scope. An empirical comparative protocol is not supplied. [REVIEWER_INTERPRETATION; E02, E04, E08, E16, E18, E22]

### D4_requirements

Reconstructed requirements are to represent different readings of the same events, distinguish social agents from physical actors and member collections, track interpretation time separately from event time, and distinguish sharing, assuming, adopting, norm applicability and compliance. These derive from the examples/principles; they are not an author-provided executable CQ suite. [REVIEWER_INTERPRETATION; E04, E07, E13, E15, E17, E18]

### D5_concepts

Eight structural positions organize descriptions, situations, concepts, entities, social agents, collections, information objects and times. Schematic/non-schematic and ground-entity distinctions are orthogonal to naive name matching. Plans/norms are descriptions, executions/applications are situations, tasks/roles are concepts, and knowledge/intentional collectives are collections. Equal current membership does not establish collective identity. [AUTHOR_REPORTED; E04, E06, E11, E16, E18, E19]

The source mixes an8-position relation with list-valued arguments, a modal statement, assumed projection schemas, binary/ternary relations and compositional definitions. Variable-scope, arity, missing links and some conditional type conflicts require explicit normalization before execution. No repaired full theory or consistency proof is claimed. ### D7_grounding

c.DnS claims independence from a specific ground ontology, but retains explicit constructive principles and can add physical agents, internal representations and information supports. Social actors, their physical realizations and the collective they act for must remain distinct. Context includes interpretation/event time, community, expressed information and epistemic viewpoint; possession of a shared description is not belief in every satisfying situation. [AUTHOR_REPORTED; E03, E04, E13, E15, E21]
